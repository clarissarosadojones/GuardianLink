# GuardianLink — Brand Identity Guide

## Brand Essence

GuardianLink is a safety-first wearable tracker for vulnerable individuals who wander or elope — special needs children and adults with dementia. The brand exists at the intersection of **medical reliability**, **parental peace of mind**, and **first-responder urgency**.

**Tagline:** *"Safety in reach."*

**Brand Personality:**
- **Trustworthy** — authoritative but never cold; medical-grade dependability
- **Calm** — reduces panic in crisis situations through clarity and simplicity
- **Swift** — designed for moments that count; every second matters
- **Caring** — warm, human-centered, non-clinical in its empathy

---

## Logo

### Primary Logo Mark
The GuardianLink logo combines two core symbols:
1. **A shield** — representing protection, safety, guardianship
2. **A link/chain-lock** — representing connection, the link between caregiver and loved one, and security

The symbol resembles a shield with a interlocking link at its base, forming a stylized "G" shape. It works at small sizes (app icon, tracker face) and large (marketing, web).

### Logo Variations
- **Full lockup** — Icon + "GuardianLink" wordmark (horizontal, for web/print)
- **Icon only** — Shield-link symbol (for app icon, tracker device, favicon)
- **Wordmark only** — Stylized "GuardianLink" text (for secondary use)

### Clear Space
Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "G" in GuardianLink around all logo variations.

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 32px (icon), 120px (full lockup)
- Print: 0.5in (icon), 1.5in (full lockup)

## Typography

### Primary Font: **Inter**
A highly legible, neutral sans-serif optimized for screens. Excellent readability at small sizes.

- **Logo / Headlines:** Inter Bold (700)
- **Subheadings:** Inter SemiBold (600)
- **Body text:** Inter Regular (400)
- **Labels / UI:** Inter Medium (500)

### Font Sizes (responsive scale)

| Usage | Desktop | Mobile |
|-------|---------|--------|
| H1 (page title) | 32px/2rem | 24px/1.5rem |
| H2 (section) | 24px/1.5rem | 20px/1.25rem |
| H3 (card title) | 18px/1.125rem | 16px/1rem |
| Body | 16px/1rem | 14px/0.875rem |
| Caption/Label | 14px/0.875rem | 12px/0.75rem |
| Small/detail | 12px/0.75rem | 11px/0.6875rem |

### Monospace: JetBrains Mono
Used for medical codes, IDs, tracker serial numbers, medication dosages.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity under pressure** — The First Responder App must be readable in 2 seconds. No ambiguity, no decorative fluff.
2. **Trust through consistency** — Every element follows the grid and color system. Predictable interfaces build trust.
3. **Warmth without condescension** — The Caregiver Dashboard is inviting and supportive, not clinical or infantilizing.
4. **Accessibility first** — Meet WCAG 2.1 AA minimum (AAA where possible). High contrast, large touch targets, readable type.

---

## Iconography

- **Style:** Line-based, rounded corners, 2px stroke weight
- **Tone:** Simple, clear, non-decorative — every icon must serve a purpose
- **Key icons needed:**
  - Shield (protection)
  - Link/chain (connection)
  - Medical profile (heart/pulse plus person)
  - Tracker device (small wearable shape)
  - Alert/bell (notifications)
  - Profile/person (caregiver and loved one)
  - Settings gear
  - Shield-check (verified safe)

## UI Component Guidelines

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Guardian Blue fill, white text, 12px border-radius
- **Secondary:** White fill, Guardian Blue border and text
- **Danger/Urgent:** Emergency Red fill, white text (used only for responder critical actions)
- **Ghost:** No fill, Guardian Blue text
- Minimum touch target: 44x44px

### Cards
- White background, subtle shadow (elevation 2), 12px border-radius
- Medical info cards in responder app may use Alert Amber left-border accent for urgency

### Form Elements
- Rounded inputs (8px), Guardian Blue focus ring
- Labels above inputs in Inter Medium
- Error states in Emergency Red

### Status Indicators
- **Safe/OK:** Green dot or checkmark
- **Alert/Attention:** Amber warning icon
- **Emergency:** Red pulse icon

## Photography & Imagery Style

- Warm, candid photography of real families — never staged clinical shots
- Diverse representation across age, ability, and ethnicity
- Soft natural lighting, warm color temperature
- Avoid pictures that make subjects look vulnerable or helpless — show empowerment

---

## Voice & Tone

### First Responder Context
- **Tone:** Direct, clear, urgent but not panicked
- **Language:** Short phrases, active voice, imperative when needed
- **Example:** "Scan tracker to view medical profile." / "Allergies: Peanut (Anaphylactic)"

### Caregiver Dashboard
- **Tone:** Supportive, reassuring, empowering
- **Language:** Warm, clear, avoids jargon
- **Example:** "Your loved one's profile is up to date." / "Need to replace a lost tracker? No problem."
