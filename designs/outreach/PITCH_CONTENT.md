## ONE-PAGER CONTENT

### Header Section
**GuardianLink** (logo — shield-link icon + wordmark)
*"Safety in reach."*

**Tagline:** A wearable safety tracker for vulnerable individuals who wander — designed for first responders, by first responders.

### The Problem
Every year, thousands of special needs children and adults with dementia wander from safe environments. When first responders arrive on scene, critical minutes are lost gathering medical history, conditions, and emergency contacts from stressed caregivers — or from individuals who cannot communicate.

**Key stat:** 49% of children with autism wander or elope. 6 in 10 people with dementia will wander at least once.

### The Solution: GuardianLink
A small, wearable tracker that:
- **Only first responders can activate** — NFC tap or Bluetooth connection on arrival
- **Instantly displays the person's full emergency medical profile** on the responder's device
- **Saves critical minutes** when every second counts

### How It Works
1. **Caregiver sets up** a secure online profile with medical conditions, medications, allergies, emergency contacts, and photo
2. **Tracker is paired** to the profile — simple serial number linkage
3. **First responder taps or connects** to the tracker on scene
4. **Emergency profile appears instantly** — no login, no password, no delay

### What First Responders See
(Reference responder app mockup)

The responder's screen shows:
- **Patient photo & name** — confirm identity immediately
- **Medical conditions** — autism, epilepsy, dementia, diabetes, etc.
- **Current medications** — names, dosages, schedules
- **Allergies** — drug allergies, anaphylactic risks, latex allergies
- **Emergency contacts** — click-to-call parents, caregivers, or facilities
- **Special considerations** — communication needs, sensory sensitivities, de-escalation guidance

### Why First Responders Need GuardianLink
- ⏱ **Save 5–15 minutes per incident** — no more radioing dispatch for records
- 🩺 **Accurate medical data** — caregiver-maintained, always up to date
- 🔒 **Secure by design** — only authorized responder devices can access profiles
- 👶 **Works with non-verbal individuals** — tracker speaks for them
- 📋 **Audit trail** — every access is logged for accountability and KPI tracking
